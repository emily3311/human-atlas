# Teaching atlas expansion — approved design

The user approved this scope on 2026-09-06 after the data audit: 2,234 meshes, 2,049 lacking Chinese mappings, 383 learning entries, 39 approximate 3D placements, and three original learning cases. These figures describe the baseline, not a professionally validated dataset.

## Independent deliverables

1. Remove the exposed surface appearance from the learning UI, retaining internal anatomy and all original geometry. Make the acupoint catalogue readable with collapsed filters, a resizable desktop divider, and an expanded reading mode. Preserve the continuous explosion slider and optional anatomy panels.
2. Replace unresolved anatomy labels with accurate, source-traceable Chinese anatomical terminology. Audit coverage across every source mesh; distinguish verified glossary equivalences from compositional translations and professional review. Explain catalogue versus 3D placement coverage and investigate expansion using authoritative location references; never fabricate calibrated positions.
3. Build a separate licensing-exam question module with proper question groups, provenance, answer explanations, and truthful question counts. Only import material with a suitable reuse basis; exclude purported past exam papers lacking authorization and other examination levels. Imported but not medically reviewed data must not be represented as reviewed. Keep existing recall cards separate from exam practice.

## Constraints

Preserve local learning records, the warm ivory/brown palette, every original mesh, original source identifiers, and the existing continuous explosion behavior. Do not convert the app into a clinical treatment guide. Do not claim complete clinical calibration or official exam endorsement. A source/access/authorization blocker must be reported explicitly, without silently redefining unfinished work as complete.
